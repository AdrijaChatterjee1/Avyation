Why this matters

Under most passenger-rights rules, airlines don’t owe compensation for genuinely extraordinary events like severe storms. That makes “weather” the easiest excuse to use, and the hardest for a stranded passenger to challenge at the gate.

Avyation does the checking for you using public airport data, and puts the evidence straight into your claim.

Questions

 

How much does it cost?

Nothing upfront. Avyation keeps 15–20% of any successful payout, and charges nothing if you don’t get paid.

Which flights are covered?

Flights governed by India’s DGCA rules, EU261 and US DOT rules, depending on the route and airline. More regions are on the roadmap.

Will you really file for me?

Yes. Once you approve the notice and confirm the details, our web agent fills in and submits the airline’s form on your behalf.

Is my data safe?

Your ticket and payout details are used only to check your flight and file your claim.

What if the airline rejects it?

We keep the evidence on file, follow up automatically, and can escalate to the regulator’s grievance portal.
